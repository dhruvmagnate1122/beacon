# Beacon launch kit

Lead with the last-mile launch problem, not with “please star my repo.”

## Positioning

**Category:** evidence-driven launch-readiness framework for web apps.

**One-line pitch:** Catch privacy, regulatory, security, and operational gaps before launch — without pretending a scan is certification.

**What makes Beacon different:** source signals stay candidates, applicability is explicit, future-effective duties stay distinct from present failures, and runtime/config/legal evidence is kept visible instead of collapsed into one compliance score.

Current executable packs:

- India DPDP;
- EU/EEA GDPR + ePrivacy;
- UK GDPR + PECR;
- selected US federal digital-product regimes: COPPA, CAN-SPAM, DMCA §512(c), ADA;
- India CERT-In;
- EU digital VAT / OSS.

## Show HN draft

**Title:** Show HN: Beacon – evidence-driven launch readiness for web apps

AI coding tools make deployment fast. The last-mile checks around privacy, consent, security, accessibility, subscriptions, cloud-cost exposure, incident readiness, and jurisdiction-specific rules are still fragmented.

I built **Beacon**, an open-source coding-agent skill + dependency-free Python readiness engine.

The design constraint is that a source regex is not a legal finding. Beacon separates source candidates, project/applicability facts, runtime/config evidence, primary-source legal review, and future-effective duties.

It currently ships six executable regulatory packs: India DPDP, EU/EEA GDPR + ePrivacy, UK GDPR + PECR, selected US federal digital-product rules, India CERT-In, and EU digital VAT/OSS.

There is no “compliance score” or certification badge. Missing evidence stays unknown, and pack applicability is explicit.

Repo: https://github.com/dhruvmagnate1122/beacon

I’d especially value criticism of the evidence model, pack boundaries, false positives, and what should *not* be automated.
